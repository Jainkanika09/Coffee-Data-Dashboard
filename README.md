Coffee Shop Sales Analytics Dashboard ☕
Project Overview

This project analyzes 3,547 coffee shop transactions to uncover patterns in customer behavior, peak sales hours, product performance, and revenue trends.

Using Power BI, Power Query, and DAX, the goal was to transform raw transaction data into actionable business insights that can help café operators optimize staffing, promotions, and product strategy.

The analysis focuses on answering key business questions:

When do customers buy coffee the most?

Which products generate the highest demand?

Which weekdays and months perform best?

How can operations be optimized using data?

Dataset Description

The dataset contains 3,547 coffee orders recorded across different times, weekdays, and months.

Key fields used in the analysis:

Column	Description
Date	Transaction date
Hour_of_Day	Hour when the order was placed
Weekday	Day of the week
Month_name	Month of transaction
Coffee_name	Coffee product purchased
Money (Rs)	Revenue from each order
Time_of_Day	Morning / Afternoon / Night
Cash_Type	Payment method

Key Business Insights
1. Morning Rush Drives Revenue

Nearly 24% of daily revenue occurs between 9 AM and 11 AM.

Peak hour revenue:

₹26K out of ₹112K total revenue.

This indicates the morning rush is the most critical business window.

Operational implication:

Staff scheduling should prioritize this period.

2. Tuesday Is the Strongest Sales Day

Tuesday generates the highest weekly revenue.

Revenue contribution:

Approximately ₹18K, making it the top-performing weekday.

Possible explanation:

Mid-week coffee demand spike.

3. Americano and Latte Dominate Demand

Top-selling products:

Coffee	Orders
Americano	809
Latte	757
Cappuccino	564

These drinks represent the core revenue drivers.

4. Espresso Underperforms

Espresso has the lowest order volume.

This suggests an opportunity for:

Product bundling

Promotional offers

Upselling strategies

5. Seasonal Sales Patterns

Monthly sales analysis shows spikes in:

March

October

These months outperform others, indicating seasonal demand patterns.

Operational Recommendations

Based on the analysis, café operators could improve performance by:

Staff Optimization

Increase staff coverage during the 9–11 AM peak window.

Promotional Strategy

Launch weekday morning combo offers to capture the morning rush.

Product Strategy

Bundle Espresso with top-selling drinks to improve its sales performance.

Example:

Americano + Espresso combo.

Demand-Based Scheduling

Prepare inventory and staffing based on hourly demand patterns.
